# YOLO × FluxQ: Operational Demonstration Walkthrough Guide

## 1. Demonstration Purpose

This guide provides an end-to-end operational script for demonstrating **YOLO × FluxQ** to logistics managers, supply chain architects, machine learning researchers, and executive stakeholders.

The scenario follows the life-cycle of **Shipment SH-2048**, an urgent pharmaceuticals shipment in transit on the **Chennai to Bengaluru arterial corridor (NH48)**.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Demonstration Script

### Step 1: Control Tower Overview (Nominal Monitoring)
1. Open the browser to `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Observe the **Global KPI Banner**:
   - Total Monitored Shipments (249 active shipments).
   - High-Risk Shipments ($R \ge 7$).
   - Predicted SLA Breach Count.
   - Active Corridor Disruption Events.
3. Review the **Risk Distribution Gauge** and the **Operational Activity Log** updating in real time.

---

### Step 2: Shipment Intelligence & TreeSHAP Explainability
1. Click the **Shipment Intelligence** tab in the navigation bar.
2. Search for or locate **`SH-2048`** (Chennai $\to$ Bengaluru, Pharmaceuticals, Priority High).
3. Observe its initial state:
   - Status: `in_transit`.
   - Risk Score: $R = 2$ (Low).
   - SLA Breach Probability: $p = 0.08$.
   - Predicted Delay: $0.0$ mins.
4. Click the **"Explain Risk"** (Eye) button on `SH-2048`.
5. The **TreeSHAP Explainability Drawer** opens:
   - Displays base value, output probability, and the top contributing factors (`sla_buffer_minutes`, `weather_severity`, `congestion_index`).
   - Highlight the regulatory disclaimer: *"SHAP values measure statistical attribution within the model. They do not constitute physical or causal proof of disruption etiology."*

---

### Step 3: Interactive Route Map View
1. Click the **Interactive Route Map** tab.
2. View the geospatial layout across South India:
   - Blue pins represent origin hubs (Chennai Port/Logistics Park).
   - Green pins represent destination fulfillment centers (Bengaluru Electronic City).
   - Shipment `SH-2048` is marked along National Highway 48 (NH48) passing near Ranipet.
   - Red warning circles depict active disruption zones.

---

### Step 4: Disruption Lab (Simulating Corridor Shock)
1. Click the **Disruption Lab** tab.
2. Select the preset scenario: **"Chennai-Bengaluru Monsoon Storm (NH48)"**.
   - Event Type: `SEVERE_WEATHER`.
   - Severity: `4.8 / 5.0`.
   - Epicenter: Vellore Corridor (12.9165°N, 79.1325°E).
   - Impact Radius: `75 km`.
   - Projected Delay: `180 minutes`.
3. Click **"Inject Disruption"**.
4. The system executes spatial corridor matching:
   - Notifies that `SH-2048` and adjacent corridor freight fall within the impact envelope.
   - Layer 2 recomputes ML risk: SLA breach probability surges above $0.60$, and risk jumps from $R = 2 \to R = 8$.
   - Early Warning Indicator (EWI) triggers, placing the shipment in `critical` review.

---

### Step 5: Recovery Center & Multi-Objective MIP Optimization
1. Click the **Recovery Center** tab.
2. Select **`SH-2048`** in the recovery workbench.
3. Review the 4 Pareto candidate plans generated by **Google OR-Tools**:
   - **Plan A (Lowest Cost):** Hold at Ranipet hub until storm passes (+₹0 INR, +180 mins delay, SLA Breached).
   - **Plan B (Lowest Delay / Expedited):** Air-freight diversion via MAA-BLR (+₹4,500 INR, 0 mins delay, On-Time).
   - **Plan C (Highest SLA Certainty):** Dedicated carrier switch (+₹2,200 INR, +15 mins delay, On-Time).
   - **Plan D (Balanced Recommendation):** Alternate arterial routing via Chittoor Expressway (+₹1,200 INR, +35 mins delay, On-Time within SLA buffer).
4. Review the **Quantum-Hybrid Benchmark Card**:
   - Displays Qiskit QAOA Statevector simulation metrics on residual slot allocation ($p=1$).
   - Shows classical baseline comparison and Quantum Contribution Ratio ($\text{QCR} = 0.0\%$).
   - Independent classical validator confirmed constraint integrity.

---

### Step 6: Human-in-the-Loop Approval & Immutable Audit Trail
1. In the Recovery Center, click **"Approve Plan"** on Plan D (Balanced Chittoor Alternate).
2. Enter Operator ID (e.g. `DISPATCHER-CHENNAI-01`) and confirm approval.
3. The platform executes atomic operational state update:
   - Shipment status transitions from `critical` to `rerouted`.
   - Route updates to `ROUTE-ALT-CHITTOOR_EXPRESSWAY`.
   - Risk score drops back to $R = 3$ (Low Risk, SLA Preserved).
4. Click the **Decision Audit** tab:
   - Verify the immutable audit log entry:
     - Event: `OPERATOR_RECOVERY_APPROVED`.
     - Timestamp, Operator ID, previous state ($R=8$), new state ($R=3$), and financial justification recorded.
